Wexford County Council, in partnership with Liam Neville Developments, is delighted to announce the launch of a new phase of 10 homes available under the Affordable Purchase Scheme at Cúl Caisleán, Taghmon, offering eligible purchasers the opportunity to buy a high-quality, energy-efficient new home at a reduced purchase price.

Located in the heart of the thriving village of Taghmon, Cúl Caisleán is an attractive new residential development designed to provide modern, comfortable living in a well-connected community. These thoughtfully designed homes combine contemporary architecture, quality finishes and excellent energy performance, making them ideal for first-time buyers and eligible home purchasers.

Taghmon offers an excellent range of local amenities including primary and secondary schools, supermarkets, cafés, pharmacy, medical centre, sporting facilities and community services, while Wexford Town is approximately 15 minutes away, providing easy access to employment, shopping, education and leisure opportunities. The N25 and M11 road networks are also within easy reach, making commuting straightforward.

Affordable Purchase Scheme -

Eligible purchasers can buy a new home at a reduced price, with Wexford County Council providing an Affordable Dwelling Contribution to bridge the gap between the market value of the home and the purchaser's maximum mortgage and deposit capacity. In return, the Council retains a percentage equity share in the property.

Application Process -

Applications for these homes must be submitted through Wexford County Council Affordable Housing Online Application Portal.

Application Portal Opens: 12.00pm Wednesday, 5th August 2026

Prospective applicants are strongly encouraged to review the eligibility requirements and supporting documentation in advance of the application period links - https://www.wexfordcoco.ie/housing/affordable-housing/local-authority-affordable-purchase-scheme

Applicant Information Events - Register Now

To help prospective applicants understand the scheme and navigate the application process, Wexford County Council are hosting in-person/online events including,

• Information Launch Event – 22nd July 2026, 7.00pm, Taghmon Action Group Centre, Taghmon, Co. Wexford.

• Application Webinar – Online Event, 29th July, 7.00pm

To register to attend any of these events please email rsvp@wexfordcoco.ie
